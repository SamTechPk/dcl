Do you provide bats and balls?

Please bring your bats and tape balls. DCL provides the arena, wickets and lighting.

Is my slot booked when I select it here?

No. This is an inquiry. Your slot is confirmed by the arena team on WhatsApp after availability and advance payment are checked.

Can I book a session that crosses midnight?

Yes. Choose your From and To times, such as 11 PM to 2 AM. Choose the date your session starts. An end time marked “Next day” falls on the following calendar day. We are open 24 hours; night cricket runs from 6 PM to 6 AM.

How much advance do I need to pay?

A minimum 50% advance is required. The remaining balance is collected at the end of play on the session day.
